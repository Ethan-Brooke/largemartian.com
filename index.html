      About

      Large Martian is a rock band from Marin County, California — San Anselmo and Fairfax are home turf. We play the songs we grew up on: David Bowie, Neil Young, Jimi Hendrix, the Velvet Underground, Radiohead, the Pixies, Tom Petty, Wilco, Gillian Welch, Old Crow Medicine Show, Bob Dylan, the Rolling Stones, Billy Idol, Joan Jett, the Cure, Simple Minds, Rihanna.
